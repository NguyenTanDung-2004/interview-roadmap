## What is Fine-Tuning?
When an AI company creates a base model, they train it on the entire internet. This is **"Pre-Trainning"**

When you fine-tune that model, you take that massive, pre-trained brain and feed it a much smaller but highly specific dataset of example. (usually thousands of pairs of inputs and perfect outputs).

*You are telling the AI: "You already know how to talk. Now, I want you to look at these 5,000 examples and learn to talk EXACTLY like this"*
