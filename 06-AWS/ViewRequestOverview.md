## Request Flow Overview

### Phase1: Turn on X-Ray Configuration of Lambda Service.
- Step1: Click on Lambda
- Step2: Move to **Configuration** Tab 
- Step3: Scroll down and click on **Monitoring and Operations tools** in the left sidebar.
- Step4: Click **Edit** button in **Additional Monitorning Tools**
- Step5: Enable **Lambda Service traces**, and then **Save**

### Phase2: View FLow in CloudWatch

- Step1: Click on Cloudwatch
- Step2: Click on **Application Signals (APM)**
- Step3: Click on **Traces** and **Trace Map** to view the overview Flow.
