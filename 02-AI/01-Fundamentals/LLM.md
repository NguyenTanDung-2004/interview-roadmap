## Large Language Models (LLMs)
- LLMs are the specific type of AI.
- The most successful and visible form of Narrow AI.

## How they actually work (The simple Version)
They don't think or know things they way human do. Instead, they are trained on massive amounts of text data (books, articles, websites, code) and learn the mathematical relationship between words.

**1. Tokenization:** When you type a prompt, the model breaks your words down into smaller pieces called tokens.

**2. The transformer Architecture:** underlyiing math structure that allows the model to look at a whole sentence at once and understand the context (understanding "bank" means a river bank or a financial bank based on the surrounding words).

**3. Next-Token Prediction:** Based on everything it learned during training, the model calculates what the most likely next token should be, outputs it, and repeats the process over and over until the response is finished.

## Key Traits of LLMs
**1. They are "Generative":** They don't just copy and paste from a database; they generate entirely new sequence of text of they fly.

**2. They are Multimodal:** process more than just text - They can natively read and generate images, audio, video and code.

**3. They are Agentic:** A major shift involves moving from passive "chatbots" to AI Agents. Modern LLMs can be give a goal, write out a multi-step plan, use tools (like web browsers or code execution environments), and autonomously complete tasks.
