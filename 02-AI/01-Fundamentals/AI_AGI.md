## AI - Artificial Intelligence / Narrow AI
Narrow AI is designed to at specific or defined tasks. It might be incredibly good at that task - even far surpassing human capabilities - but it cannot apply its intelligence to anything outside its programming. 
- How it works: It relies on data, algorithms, and machine learning to find the patterns and make predictions or generations.

## AGI - Artificial General Intelligence
AGI often called "Strong AI" is a theoriacal form of AI that prossesses human-level intellect. AGI wouldn't just good at one thing, it would have the capacity to understand, learn and apply knowledge across an incredibly wide range of tasks, much like a human adult.

## Differences
- **Scope of Ability:**
    + AI: Highly specialized; limited to specific tasks.
    + AGI: Broad; adaptable to almost any intellectual human task.
- **Learning Curve:**
    + AI: Require massive, specific datasets to learn one new skills.
    + AGI: Can learn from small amounts of data and transfer skills across domains.
- **Problem Solving:**
    + AI: Fails or Hallucinates when pushed outside its specific training boundaries.
    + AGI: Can reason through entirely novel situations it has never seen before.
- **Existence:**
    + AI: Present
    + AGI: Future. Estimates on arrival range from a few years to decades.
