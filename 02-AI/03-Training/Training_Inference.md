## Training
- **What it is:** The initial - incredibly expensive phase where a model learns from a massive dataset.
- **Why it matters:** This is where the model reads billions of pages of text to understand grammar, facts, reasoning patterns, and human language. 

## Inference
- **What it is:** The phase where the already-trained goes to work and processes live requests.
- **Why it matters:** Everytime you type a prompt into a chatbot and it generates a response, the model is running "inference". It is not learning anything new or changing its core brain; It is applying its pre-existing training to compute an answer for you in real-time.
